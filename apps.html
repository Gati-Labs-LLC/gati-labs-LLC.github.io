Two coaches. One way of thinking.

OwlyGo and Rucktoise coach different things, the same way. Each reads your recovery every morning, suggests one move, and lets you have the final say.

OwlyGo

The coach that knows when to push, and when to ease off.

For busy people who want an active week: strength at a gym or at home, plus walking, running, cycling, swimming, yoga and Flow.

  • A move for today. Each morning Owly reads last night’s recovery and suggests one move, with the weights and reps worked out on strength days.
  • Guided, step by step. Warm-ups, sets and timed rests, spoken cues, and a calm cooldown, on iPhone and Apple Watch.
  • A week that bends. Busy Tuesday? Move the workout to Thursday. Your week stays balanced.
  • Fits your kit. A pair of dumbbells, a few bands or a full gym.
  • Works with what you wear. Apple Watch, Oura or WHOOP, through Apple Health.

Made for busy humans.

Rucktoise

Ruck like a tortoise. Slow, steady and strong.

Rucking is walking with a little weight, in a backpack or a weighted vest. No gym, no running, no strict routine.

  • Today’s ruck, suggested. A Green, Yellow or Red status each morning, with the weight, the time, the terrain and a heart-rate ceiling to stay under.
  • A tap when it matters. Your Apple Watch shows your heart rate against today’s ceiling, and taps your wrist if you go over.
  • A plan that adapts. Rate your effort and note any pain after each ruck. Report pain, and the plan holds or steps the weight down.
  • Build steadily. Time first, then weight, a little at a time, and only when you say yes.
  • Start with what you have. Any sturdy backpack, or a vest with adjustable weights.
