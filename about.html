Built for the long run.

Why we exist

Most fitness plans treat every day the same. Real weeks don’t work that way.

A short night, a busy Tuesday or a sore knee changes what today should be.

Gati Labs builds coaches that start with how you are today. They read your recovery each morning, suggest one sensible move, and ease off when you need it, so you keep coming back.

How we build

  • Rules you can follow. Our apps run on fixed, written rules, not an AI model. The same readings always give the same plan, and the app tells you why.
  • Grounded in guidance. Quiet Fitness workouts follow the American College of Sports Medicine guidelines, and its suggested week follows the Physical Activity Guidelines for Americans.
  • Conservative by default. When in doubt, our apps suggest the easier path. Weights and loads rise slowly, and only when you say yes.
  • Private by design. Everything runs on your iPhone and Apple Watch. No account, no server, no analytics, no ads.
  • Calm, not pushy. No streaks, no leaderboards, no guilt. Nothing celebrates on a hard day.

The name

Gati means momentum, or pace.

That’s the idea: steady momentum, at a pace you can keep.
