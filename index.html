What both apps share

Your night sets the day.

They read sleep, heart rate variability and resting heart rate from Apple Health, and compare them with your own usual.

One clear suggestion.

A simple Green, Yellow or Red status, and one move for today. On a harder morning, the plan gets lighter, and the app says why.

You decide.

Weights and loads only go up when you say yes. Swap or skip anything. Report pain, and the plan never gets harder.

Rest counts.

No streaks to lose, no red rings. A rest day is part of the plan.

Private by design.

No account, no server, no AI model. Your readings stay on your iPhone and Apple Watch.

Steady wins.

We build for the long run: plans that bend around a busy week, so you keep showing up.
